How the fire works

Every area of your life is a fire. It cools on its own, and small actions heat it back up. The design makes one thing obvious at a glance: what’s burning, and what needs feeding now.

Three places an area can live

Main

One focus at a time. Cools every 2–3 hours during your active hours, so it asks for several 5–10 minute sparks a day. By evening you’re warmed up for a big session.

On the side

Kept alive, not pushed. Each has its own cooling speed: daily, every few days, or weekly. One small action a cycle keeps them warm.

Archive

Paused on purpose and frozen. Archived areas don’t cool or nag. History is kept, and they can move back any time.

Cooling

Heat drops one level per cooling period. The main fire’s clock only runs inside your active hours (default 8:00–23:00), so it never dies overnight. Side areas count whole days.

Heating

A spark (5–10 minutes) lifts a fire one level. A big session takes it straight to hot and is marked in history, so sparks leading into evening sessions become visible.

Layout

Spacing

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48. Screen edge 16, card padding 20, gap between cards 12.

Radius

12 for icon buttons, 24 for cards and sheets, full pill for buttons, chips and tabs. Fires are perfect circles.

Light and depth

Fires get a glowing white rim. Cards are either raised glass or sunken navy, never drop shadows.

Feedback

Feeding a fire plays one short flare: the rim brightens, the core swells, then settles at the new level.

Reminders

“JavaScript is down to embers.” Sent only when a fire drops a level, and only inside active hours.

Empty states

No areas yet: “Name the first thing you want to keep warm.” A cold fire shows its smallest spark, never a guilt message.

Open decisions

Whether ambient mode is on by default, the exact active hours, and whether more than one area can be main at once.

Style note

The bright sky, glass cards and glowing ring follow the feel of fitness reward apps. The 3D fire is Microsoft’s Fluent Emoji (MIT licence, free for commercial use). No Sweatcoin logos, artwork or marks are used.
